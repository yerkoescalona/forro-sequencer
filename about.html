About

A drum sequencer for forró rhythms — built for musicians, students, and anyone curious about the music of northeastern Brazil.

About me

I'm Yerko Escalona, originally from Chile, currently based in Vienna, Austria. I got into forró through dancing about four years ago and quickly fell in love with the music. That love led me to pick up the zabumba, and today I play it in Beira Mar (@beira.mar.forro), a forró band here in Vienna.

I built this sequencer because I wanted a tool to study and visualize the percussion patterns that drive forró — something I could use to practice, teach, and share rhythms with other musicians and dancers. As a software developer, building it myself felt like the natural thing to do.

You can find me on Instagram at @yerkoescalona.

What is forró?

Forró is a family of music genres and partner dances from the northeast of Brazil.

At its core, forró is powered by a trio of percussion instruments: the zabumba, the pandeiro, and the triângulo.

The genre was popularized by Luiz Gonzaga (1912–1989), the "King of Baião."

The instruments

Zabumba

A large double-headed bass drum played with a mallet (maceta) on one side and a thin stick (bacalhau) on the other.

Pandeiro

A frame drum with jingles, similar to a tambourine but played with sophisticated technique.

Triângulo

The triangle in forró is not the simple timekeeper of orchestral music — it plays a driving, syncopated pattern.

What this sequencer does

The Forró Sequencer lets you build, hear, and tweak traditional forró percussion patterns step by step. It is designed for:

  • Forró musicians — practice rhythms, experiment with variations.
  • Dance students — hear isolated instruments to understand the musical structure.
  • Music teachers — demonstrate rhythm patterns visually and audibly.
  • Curious people — explore the percussion of northeastern Brazil interactively.

All sounds are synthesized in real-time using the Web Audio API — no samples to load, no downloads. The sequencer works entirely in your browser.

Built with
  • Vanilla JavaScript — ES modules, no frameworks
  • Web Audio API — sample-accurate scheduling and real-time synthesis
  • Vite — development server and production builds
  • Vitest — unit testing
  • GitHub Pages — hosting
